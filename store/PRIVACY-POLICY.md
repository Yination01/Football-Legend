# Privacy Policy — Football Legend

**Effective date:** September 2, 2026

Football Legend ("the game") is developed and published by Yination ("we", "us").
This policy explains what data the game handles.

## The short version

**Football Legend collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device.**

## Details

### Data we do NOT collect
- No account, registration, email, or login of any kind
- No name, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
- No data is transmitted to us or to any third party

### Data stored on your device only
The game stores your progress locally on your device:
- Game save data (careers, squads, settings) in the app's private storage
- A backup copy of your save in your device's `Documents/FootballLegend` folder, so your progress survives if you uninstall and reinstall the game
- An error log (technical messages only, no personal information), kept on your device, which you can view, copy, or clear at any time in Settings. It is only shared if you personally choose to send it to us.

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting your careers in the game's Settings, deleting the `Documents/FootballLegend` folder, or uninstalling the app.

### Permissions
- **Storage (Android 10 and older only):** used solely to write the save-backup file described above. Never used to read your other files.

### Android Auto Backup
Your device may back up the game's data to your own Google account as part of Android's standard app backup. This is controlled by your device settings, not by us, and we have no access to it.

### Children
The game contains no chat, no social features, no data collection, and no third-party advertising, and is suitable for a general audience.

### Purchases
The game currently contains no in-app purchases. If this changes, purchases will be processed by Google Play and this policy will be updated.

### Changes
If we ever change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before the change takes effect.
